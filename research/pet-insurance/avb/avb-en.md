# General Terms and Conditions (AVB): Accident & Illness Insurance for Dogs and Cats

**Pawse Demo Pet Insurance (fictional) · Edition December 2025 (demo)**

> FICTIONAL demo terms for the Pawse hackathon. This is an English adaptation, shortened and paraphrased, of the
> Calingo Insurance AG AVB "Unfall- & Krankenversicherung für Hunde und Katzen", edition December 2025
> (risk carrier Allianz Suisse). Section numbers follow the original; the sub-numbers 1.2.1 to 1.2.9 are ours
> (the original lists those headings without numbers). The German original is the only binding text of the real product.
> See [`../README.md`](../README.md#sources) for provenance.

The cover you chose, the sums insured, the deductible and the reimbursement rate are listed in your **policy schedule**
([`../policy/policy.json`](../policy/policy.json)). A coverage that is not selected in the schedule is not insured.

## 1 Insurance cover

### 1.1 Insured animals
The dogs and cats named in the policy schedule.

### 1.2 Insured risks: base package
All treatments must be recommended **and** performed by a vet. We do not pay for treatments you request yourself or
that a vet did not prescribe or perform. Vets are reimbursed according to the reference tariff.

We recommend a vet check-up at least every 12 months (including a dental check) and following the vet's vaccination
and treatment recommendations. If you skip these and there is a link to the treated condition, benefits may be reduced.

#### 1.2.1 General veterinary treatment for accident and illness
- We pay for treatment the responsible vet recommends.
- **Excluded:** cosmetic and reconstructive surgery for aesthetic reasons, and its consequences.

#### 1.2.2 Dental treatment
- We pay for dental treatment the vet orders because of dental disease or an accident.
- Crowns are only paid when needed because of an accident.
- **Excluded:** treatment that an annual dental check would have prevented.

#### 1.2.3 Preventive treatment
After the **annual deductible is used up**, we pay up to the prevention sum insured for:
(a) vaccinations; (b) neutering/castration; (c) dental scaling; (d) flea and worm treatments; (e) the annual vet check-up;
(f) senior check-ups (dogs from 8, cats from 10 years). No other preventive treatment is covered.

#### 1.2.4 Emergency pet care
- If you or a family member must be admitted to hospital **in an emergency**, we pay for boarding your pet in a cattery/kennel
  or with a pet sitter.
- **Excluded:** pet care during a **planned** hospital stay.

#### 1.2.5 Emergency treatment
- If the vet decides treatment outside opening hours is necessary, we pay the extra emergency costs.
- Extra costs of a **house call** are only covered if your annual accident-and-illness sum insured is at least **CHF 12'000**.
- **Excluded:** out-of-hours costs where the vet considers the treatment could have waited until normal opening hours.

#### 1.2.6 Complementary medicine (only if selected)
Hydrotherapy; homeopathic or herbal medicines; acupuncture; physiotherapy; osteopathy; chiropractic; laser therapy,
when needed and recommended and performed by a qualified therapist or vet.

#### 1.2.7 Advanced treatments (only if selected)
Stem-cell treatment, gene therapy and prostheses, when the vet recommends it and all other options are exhausted.
No other advanced treatments are covered.

#### 1.2.8 Behavioural medicine (only if selected)
Behavioural treatment after referral by your vet to a behavioural specialist (STVV member or equivalent).
- **Excluded:** conditions that the specialist attributes to poor training; animals not kept in a species-appropriate way under
  the Swiss Animal Welfare Act (TSchG/TSchV).

#### 1.2.9 Prescription diet
- We pay for prescription-only food the vet prescribes as part of treating an illness or injury.
- **Excluded:** food prescribed for weight loss; food supplements, even if prescribed by a vet.

### 1.3 Add-on: pre-existing conditions (only if selected)
Up to the agreed sum, we pay for treatment of pre-existing illnesses and accidents for which the animal was treated or diagnosed
before the policy started.
- **Excluded:** pre-existing conditions first diagnosed or treated within **three months** before the policy started, and their follow-up treatment.

### 1.4 General exclusions
We pay no benefits for:
- **a.** treatments and operations not ordered and performed by a vet or recognised specialist;
- **b.** (reduction or refusal) where there is a link between a missed annual check-up or an ignored vaccination/treatment
  recommendation and the accident or illness;
- **c.** animals younger than 8 weeks;
- **d.** illness, hereditary diseases and congenital defects during the **30-day waiting period** from the policy start, including all
  **follow-up treatment** of those conditions. No waiting period for accidents or prevention;
- **e.** pre-existing conditions diagnosed or treated before the policy started, and their consequences (unless covered under 1.3);
- **f.** vet reports and certificates issued at your request;
- **g.** registration and microchipping;
- **h.** any cost caused directly or indirectly by your negligent or intentional act or omission;
- **i.** accidents at competitive events (races, coursing); accidents in training are covered;
- **j.** treatment not based on recognised medical or complementary practice;
- **k.** vet costs that are not reasonable and customary (deviation from the reference tariff);
- **l.** diet treatment, treatment of illness caused by overweight, and food or medicine for that purpose, unless the weight gain results from a diagnosed illness;
- **m.** costs related to reproduction, e.g. pregnancy, birth, infertility, genetic tests;
- **n.** injuries caused by third parties or other animals. **But** if the third party has no liability insurance or cannot be identified, we pay;
- **o.** your animal injuring other animals or people, or damaging third-party property (this is a liability matter);
- **p.** benefits that would breach applicable sanctions;
- **q.** consequences of war, riots, nuclear events, epidemics and pandemics, terrorism.

### 1.5 Benefits
1. **Compensation.** We reimburse the necessary vet costs up to the sums insured in the schedule. You share costs through a cost contribution;
   the treatment date is decisive. Reimbursement is in CHF.
2. **Annual deductible.** The agreed deductible applies first; any limit applies only after the deductible is used up.
   *Original example:* limit 6'000, deductible 1'000, 100% rate. Case 1 costs 4'000: insurer pays 3'000, you pay 1'000.
   Case 2 costs 600 later that year: deductible already used, insurer pays 600.
3. **Reimbursement rate and per-condition excess.** The agreed rate applies after the deductible/excess, then the limit.
   *Original example:* excess 300, rate 80%, costs 1'000: insurer pays (1'000 − 300) × 80% = 560, you pay 440.

## 2 Submitting a vet visit
- Submit the vet invoice as soon as possible, **at the latest within 30 days**; late submission can reduce benefits.
- Required: original invoice with first-diagnosis date, treatment date, animal name, diagnosis, services and medicines, amounts,
  and the vet's name, address and phone; receipts and prescriptions. The insurer may request the medical history and ask the vet directly.
- The insurer may require an independent vet examination at its own cost (travel costs reimbursed up to CHF 200).
- You owe the vet the invoiced amount unless a direct-payment arrangement exists.

## 3 General provisions
- **3.1 Territory:** worldwide, at the local reference tariff. Cover ends at the end of the month in which you move your residence out of Switzerland.
- **3.2 Time:** only events occurring during the contract term, subject to waiting periods: (a) accident and prevention from day one;
  (b) illness: nothing for diagnoses or treatments in the first 30 days, nor their follow-up treatments;
  (c) pre-existing add-on: nothing for conditions first diagnosed or treated within 3 months before the start.
- **3.3 Premiums:** after a reminder, 14 days to pay. If unpaid, cover is suspended from the day after the deadline until everything is paid;
  illnesses or accidents occurring or treated during suspension **stay excluded** after payment. Reminder fee up to CHF 30.
- **3.4 Duration:** one year, renewing tacitly. Either party may cancel at any time, effective at the end of the month; either party may cancel after a claim.
  The contract ends on the animal's death.
- **3.5 Changes:** coverage can be changed for the next period (notice 14 days before). If a treatment was refused for lack of cover, that condition
  counts as **pre-existing** after an upgrade.
- **3.6 Law:** Swiss law, Swiss courts.

## 4 Main obligations
### 4.1 Owner
(a) Comply with the Swiss Animal Welfare Act; (b) vet check-up every 12 months (including teeth);
(c) if the animal is ill or injured, have a vet examine it without delay and follow the treatment recommendations.

### 4.2 Policyholder
- **a. Claims:** submit the invoice promptly; allow investigations; answer truthfully and completely.
- **b. Change in risk:** if any fact stated in the schedule changes (e.g. **indoor cat becomes outdoor cat**, moving abroad), notify the insurer in writing without delay.
- **c. Police report:** injury by third parties or third-party animals, and malicious acts, must be reported to the police without delay.
- **d. Mitigation:** follow the insurer's instructions to reduce the loss.
- **e. Recourse:** provide information needed for recourse against third parties.

**Breach:** intentional breach of 4.1 or 4.2 frees the insurer from paying. Grossly negligent breach allows a reduction proportional to fault;
you must prove you were not grossly negligent.

## 5 Definitions
- **Accident:** sudden, unintended, harmful effect of an unusual external factor on the animal's body, impairing its health and requiring vet treatment.
- **Illness:** any impairment of physical health that is not the result of an accident and requires vet examination or treatment.
- **Emergency:** medically necessary, unforeseen, unplanned treatment that cannot be delayed without seriously endangering health.
- **Follow-up treatment:** a medical measure that is the direct consequence of an earlier treatment or diagnosis
  (e.g. surgery for a broken leg on 01.01, physiotherapy for that leg on 01.02).
- **Pre-existing condition:** an illness or accidental injury that occurred before the start of cover, was already recognisable, or could have been
  diagnosed by a vet at an examination, and all resulting follow-up treatment.
- **Hereditary disease:** a disease occurring in families or through new mutations; a genetic predisposition counts as a hereditary disease.
- **Annual deductible:** fixed amount you pay per year toward vet costs (including prevention) before the insurer pays. Not applicable to the pre-existing add-on,
  which uses a per-condition excess.
- **Reimbursement rate:** share of each invoice the insurer pays (80% rate = 20% co-payment).
- **Waiting period:** period after the start of cover during which no benefits are paid.
- **Family member:** spouse or partner, children, parents, grandparents, and people living in the same household.
- **Reference tariff:** the customary fees a vet practice charges for a treatment.
